Content (HAL record `insu-02562440`, abstract field retrieved via the HAL

of this paper we noticed some mistakes in the a priori deep moonquake
locations used for waveform data analysis (Table 2), and an error in the

Adjudication: the draft cites Garcia et al. (2011) at exactly one point —
§ 5.3, for the lunar core-radius estimate ("independent estimates near
330–380 km"). The erratum corrects moonquake locations and the pressure
profile; it does not touch the core radius (380 ± 40 km) or any
core-related seismic parameter. The cited claim is unaffected.

Action taken: a parenthetical note appended to the Garcia reference entry
(same pattern as the Bi Author Correction note), stating the erratum's
scope and its non-effect on the cited value. No co-citation in the body
text required. The ref-verify "deferred to submission assembly" flag for
this item is discharged.

Residual: full erratum text remains paywalled; the adjudication rests on

correction scope in its own words. If the full text is later acquired and
